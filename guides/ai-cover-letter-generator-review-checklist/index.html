AI Cover Letter Generator Review Checklist

AI can help you create a cover letter quickly, but the draft still needs review. A short checklist catches the mistakes that make generated letters feel generic.

Check Accuracy First

Remove any claim that is not true. AI drafts can overstate experience, invent motivations, or add skills you did not provide.

Your cover letter should be confident, but it must be accurate.

Add Job-Specific Detail

A good letter mentions the role, the kind of work, and why your background fits. Replace vague phrases with details from the job description.

Tighten the Tone

Remove excessive enthusiasm, buzzwords, and repeated adjectives. The strongest cover letters sound direct and professional.

  • Cut generic opening lines.
  • Use one clear example.
  • Keep the closing simple.
