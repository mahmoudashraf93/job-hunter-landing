Job Application Autofill

How to Autofill Job Applications on iPhone

To autofill a supported job application on iPhone, save your details in JobHunter, enable its Safari extension, open the employer’s application in Safari, and choose Autofill. Check every filled field and document before you submit. JobHunter does not submit the application for you.

Published 2026-05-28 · Updated 2026-10-19 · By the JobHunter team

What you need before starting

Install JobHunter and open it to prepare your profile. Save your name, email, phone number and any links you intend to use. Check these details against your resume, especially the email address an employer should use to reach you. Choose your current resume if you want it available during applications.

Begin with a role you have read and want to apply for. You will still need the employer’s account login when requested, your own answers to eligibility questions, and any documents or work samples listed in the application. Autofill support depends on the website and the controls it uses.

Enable JobHunter in Safari

Apple’s extension settings control whether JobHunter can work on a site. Turning on Safari’s contact AutoFill is a different setting: it uses your contact card and does not enable the JobHunter extension. If a menu label differs on your iOS version, use the Apple instructions in the sources below.

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions. On older iOS versions, Safari may appear directly in Settings.
  2. Select JobHunter and turn on Allow Extension. If you use Safari profiles, enable it for the profile where you apply.
  3. Open the employer’s application page in Safari. From the Page Menu beside the address field, open Manage Extensions if JobHunter is not available.
  4. When Safari asks about website access, choose access appropriate to the application site you are using. Return to the form after enabling the extension.

Fill the application and review it in order

Saved contact details and profile links are useful starting points. Education sections, work-history repeaters, custom controls and written questions vary across employers; do not assume they will all fill. Keep your resume open as a reference and enter unsupported sections manually.

  1. Open the actual application form. A job description page or a page inside another app’s browser may not expose the fields JobHunter needs.
  2. Open JobHunter from Safari’s extension menu and choose Autofill. Wait for the operation to finish before editing or navigating away.
  3. Compare the filled contact details with your saved profile. Correct any mismatch and complete fields that stayed empty.
  4. Check dropdowns, dates, eligibility questions and document attachments separately. A completion message is a prompt to review the result, not proof that every required answer is correct.
  5. Read the employer’s final review and consent text. Submit the application yourself only after the form and documents are ready.

Check resumes and cover letters as separate steps

A filled email field does not confirm that a resume was attached. Look at the upload control, confirm the intended filename, and wait for any processing or validation to finish. Use the file types and size limit the employer requests. If resume parsing populates other fields, compare those values with your document.

Selecting or uploading a file is different from submitting the whole application. A website may receive a document as soon as it uploads, before the final Submit step. Confirm that you trust the destination and have chosen the correct version. Review any AI-written answer or cover letter before adding it to the form.

Troubleshoot an empty or incorrect field

Keep a copy of written answers before reloading a page; reloading can discard unsaved work. If a control remains unsupported, complete it manually rather than repeatedly running autofill. For a reproducible problem, use JobHunter’s support option with the site name and a description of the affected field; avoid sending your resume or private answers unless needed.

Final review before you submit

Use autofill to reduce repeat typing while keeping your attention on the decisions that matter for this application. After submission, save the employer’s confirmation and update your application tracker. A populated form alone is not a submission receipt.

Frequently asked questions

Does Safari AutoFill fill an entire job application?

Safari’s contact AutoFill and a job-application extension are separate tools. JobHunter can fill supported fields from your saved details, but every form needs review and some sections require manual entry.

Why does JobHunter leave a field blank?

The saved value may be missing, site access may be disabled, or the website may use an unsupported control. Check the profile and permissions, then enter the answer manually if needed.

Does tapping Autofill submit my application?

No. Filling fields and attaching documents do not complete the employer’s final submission. Review the application and choose Submit yourself; a site may upload attachments before that final step.
