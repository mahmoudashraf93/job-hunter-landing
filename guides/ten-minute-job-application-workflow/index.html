A 10-Minute Job Application Workflow

A fast application should still be thoughtful. This 10-minute workflow helps you decide whether to apply, tailor the most important details, and finish the form efficiently.

Minute 1-2: Decide if the Role Is Worth It

Check responsibilities, location, compensation if listed, work authorization requirements, and must-have qualifications. Do not spend time on roles that clearly do not fit.

Minute 3-5: Choose Resume and Key Angle

Pick the closest resume version and identify the top two reasons you fit the role. If needed, adjust the summary or a top bullet before applying.

Minute 6-8: Autofill and Attach

Use JobHunter to fill repeat fields from your saved profile and attach the right resume from your iPhone. This is where the biggest time savings happen.

Minute 9-10: Review and Submit

Check required fields, resume file name, knockout questions, and any written responses. Submit only after the application matches the role and your actual experience.
